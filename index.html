Wonder Bits Daily Shorts

A short, fact-checked explainer every day on the YouTube channel Wonder Bits Clips (@wonderbitsclips): science, history, nature and other curious topics, each in under a minute.

How the videos are made

  1. A topic is chosen from popular and evergreen subjects, avoiding repeats.
  2. A script is written with AI assistance and checked claim by claim against a cited source, usually Wikipedia.
  3. Images come from Wikimedia Commons under public-domain or attribution licences, with credits in the description.
  4. Narration is AI text-to-speech; captions are added automatically.
  5. A person reviews every video and approves it before it is uploaded with the YouTube API.
