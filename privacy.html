Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Wonder Bits Daily Shorts ("the App") is a private tool operated by the owner of the Wonder Bits YouTube channel. It produces one educational YouTube Short per day for the YouTube channel Wonder Bits and uploads it only after the channel owner approves it. The App has a single user, the channel owner. It does not offer accounts to the public.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information obtained through YouTube API Services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

The App does not access or store any information about viewers, commenters, subscribers or other YouTube users. It does not read comments, messages or private channel data.

How information is used and shared

  • Information from YouTube API Services is used only to upload the owner's approved videos and to report their performance to the owner.
  • Information from YouTube API Services is not sold or shared with any third party and is not used for advertising.
  • To make videos, the App sends non-personal content to service providers: topic and script text to Anthropic (script writing) and OpenAI (voice narration), video layouts to Creatomate (rendering), and image searches to Wikimedia Commons. None of this includes YouTube API data or personal information about any user.
  • Status emails (for example "ready for review" or "published") are sent to the owner through Gmail.

Cookies and device storage

The App's review page does not set cookies, use tracking, or store information on the visitor's device.

Retention and deletion

YouTube statistics and upload responses are deleted automatically after 30 days. Video IDs of the owner's own uploads are kept for record keeping until the owner deletes the App's data. Deleting the App's data folder removes everything the App stored.

Revoking access

You can revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time on the Google security settings page (Third-party apps with account access). After you revoke access, the App can no longer upload or read anything.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
